# Transfer and conversation-continuity evidence boundaries

## What this program measures

I01, D01/H01, feature, migration, adoption and publishing tasks use independent
functional graders over the same Bingo business application. They are multiple
task families, not multiple independently sampled applications. The D01
neighbor checks are resource-scoped, not proof of two full app/worktree targets.
The separate X01 case changes both logical names and the actual failure family
from paused writes to ACL-denied writes. Its results must be recorded before
claiming fault/name transfer.

Single attempts per unique condition keep the pilot low-budget and avoid
re-running identical benchmarks. They cannot establish population success rates,
confidence in small latency differences, or model/app generalization. A sample
of one supplies no useful empirical within-condition variance for a power
calculation; do not manufacture one.

## eShop inspection, 2026-11-05

Read-only GitHub inspection pinned `dotnet/eShop` main to
`913acea66708a59595351174cee9b29f37179d29`. Its README describes .NET 10 with Aspire,
and its actual AppHost contains RabbitMQ, Redis, multiple PostgreSQL databases,
identity, catalog, basket, ordering/payment processors, YARP and web clients.
That is a plausible future asynchronous-ordering/trace-correlation transfer
target when those capabilities are the research question.

The inspected tree has AppHost/ServiceDefaults but no ready application
`docker-compose`/`compose.yaml` raw counterpart. This does not prove that no
historical/raw version exists; it means a behavior-equivalent pair was **not
verified** by this inspection. The older .NET 8 release link is not evidence
of an equivalent non-Aspire version.

Do not compare unrelated eShopOnContainers source or a different framework
release and attribute every change to Aspire. A future paired eShop experiment
needs exact business source, infrastructure/telemetry parity, dependency pins,
independent order/payment/event outcomes and adoption/setup accounting.
No eShop checkout, model run, cloud deployment or billable resource was created.
Bingo still supports the current bounded roadmap, so a large second application
is not silently added to a low-budget pilot.

## Promotion and budget decision

The executable pilot can establish scenario feasibility, concrete tool/workflow
failure modes, source-contract fixes and promising interactions. Broad product
claims require held-out applications and models with a preregistered sampling/
precision target. Preserve null/negative observations; do not spend on a repeat
sweep merely to make a positive chart. No statistical superiority or all-agent
claim is made by this program's current evidence.
